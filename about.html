About Scale To Sky

We turn ideas into brands that matter.

Founded by a small group of strategists and designers who got tired of watching good products lose to worse ones with better positioning.

Our story

Started as a two-person side project in 2018.

Northfield began when our founders, both in-house at competing consumer brands, kept running into the same problem: great products with marketing that undersold them. They started freelancing together on weekends, then left their jobs entirely once the client list outgrew the spare time.

Eight years later, we're still deliberately small. Every project is led by a senior strategist and senior designer who stay with you from the first workshop to the final launch — never handed off to a bench of juniors halfway through.

We've worked with seed-stage startups and 40-year-old family businesses alike. The size of the company matters less to us than whether the leadership is ready to make a real decision about who they are.

Our mission

We believe most companies don't have a marketing problem — they have a clarity problem. Our job is to find the true, specific thing your business does better than anyone else, and build every part of your brand around saying it well.

What we value

Four things we won't compromise on.

Creativity

Original thinking over borrowed templates, even when the safe option is easier to sell internally.

Precision

Details compound. We'd rather ship two weeks later and get the small things right.

Collaboration

The best work happens when your team and ours argue about ideas, not egos.

Growth

Design that doesn't move a real number for your business isn't finished yet.
